# Creative brief — A little life. A lifetime of care.

## Phase 1: Research and positioning
Build a complete ten-page, frontend-only React website for Dr Aditya Nimbkar, Fetal and Reproductive Medicine expert and High-Risk Obstetrics specialist, serving Thane, Mulund and Kalyan. The initial brief requested a demo; the client subsequently explicitly approved the full ten-page frontend.

Public sources reviewed: https://theovacare.com/dr-aditya-nimbkar/ ; https://www.practo.com/thane/doctor/aditya-nimbkar-1 ; https://share.google/1vuK1voWsPZVKwgFV ; https://youtube.com/@dradityanimbkar ; competitor sites https://orrchidfertilitycentre.com/ and https://www.goodnewsfertility.com/ . Instagram and Facebook access was blocked; do not imply those feeds were analysed. The named Sejal Kulkarni reference could not be confidently identified. This is focused qualitative research, not exhaustive market research or measured conversion evidence.

The local comparison shows service-heavy fertility positioning, doctor credentials, emotional reassurance and direct contact as common patterns. Differentiate through continuity of care: conception, fetal assessment, pregnancy, and lifelong gynaecological health. Let a patient's question lead the service navigation. Give transparent supplied consultation prices and one easy appointment-enquiry flow. Never promise pregnancy, a particular clinical outcome, or response times.

Use the client's supplied 7 years experience and ₹1,000 initial / ₹500 follow-up rates; public Practo data differs, so make clear these are consultation fees and other procedures vary. 3,500+ deliveries describes teams he was part of, NOT solo deliveries. 2,000+ scans and 20 publications are supplied figures. Retain authentic supplied review excerpts without invented ratings, dates or verification badges. Only the Thane Ova clinic has a confirmed address; Mulund and Kalyan locations are arranged by the team. Public hospital 24-hour status must not be presented as the doctor's consultation availability.

## Phase 2: Final implementation prompt
Create a distinct, restrained luxury editorial healthcare experience: compassionate without childish motifs, authoritative without cold clinical blues. Palette: warm ivory #FCF8F4, cocoa #44352F, baby pink #F5E5E1, muted rose #A76E62, pale gold #C5A76F, sage #7D8774. Pair Cormorant Garamond expressive serif with Manrope precise sans-serif. Avoid generic SaaS card grids, neon effects and 3D gimmicks. Use slender rules, numbered editorial chapters, generous asymmetrical spacing, art-directed portrait/arched photography and rounded CTAs sparingly.

Home: slim utility ribbon; refined wordmark navigation; large kinetic hero with three masked lines ('For every / little heartbeat. / And you.'); photographic maternity arch, fine concentric outlines and an authentic doctor portrait card; evidence strip; four numbered specialties; doctor narrative; slow typographic manifesto marquee; a three-step patient journey; real supplied testimonials; transparent fee/FAQ preview; three-location invitation; warm final consultation CTA; comprehensive footer.

Ten pages: Home, Meet the Doctor, Fetal Medicine, Pregnancy Care, Fertility Care, Gynaecology, Patient Stories, Patient Resources, Locations, Contact & Appointments. Each detail page has useful original editorial structure, supplied facts, accessible FAQs and clear next steps. Resources include six educational reading dialogs, filter/search, consultation checklist download, and a genuine external YouTube channel link, without inventing available videos. Add a browsable image gallery to About, with actual doctor portrait and clearly illustrative maternity photography.

Use Framer Motion masked staggered hero reveals, viewport section entrances, hover details, and small scroll-driven photo parallax. Use Lenis smooth desktop scrolling. Provide reduced-motion support and never hide content permanently while waiting for animation. All controls must work on touch and keyboard; no horizontal overflow.

Implement one reusable Shadcn booking dialog: name, Indian mobile number, location, service, optional preferred date and explicit consent to share these details with WhatsApp. Generate a user-reviewed message and an actual WhatsApp link. Clearly explain no appointment is confirmed until the team replies, no information is saved by this frontend and the user must send the message in WhatsApp. Do not collect clinical histories. Contact, telephone, email, social profiles and confirmed-clinic Maps must be real outbound links. No fictional live chat, payment, calendar or live social/review integration. Use a frontend privacy dialog explaining hand-off services.

Deliver proper ten-page routes, route-specific metadata, responsive navigation, active links, resource search/filters, testimonial filters, location selectors, accessible accordions and dialogs, scroll-to-top and safe homepage redirect for unknown links. Validate all flows, reduced-motion, image loading, mobile layout and production compilation.

## Phase 3: Verification and content sign-off
Verify frontend flows and identify rather than fabricate unavailable content. Before public use, obtain confirmation of all fees, doctor photo rights, permission to publish patient excerpts, medical editorial review, precise Mulund/Kalyan addresses and clinic schedules. Future additions after separate approval: live scheduling, payments, managed content and feed integrations.
